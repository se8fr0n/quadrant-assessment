About the Club

Quadrant Coding & Robotics Club is a beginner-friendly space where kids and teens learn programming and robotics through play, projects, and teamwork. From dragging colourful blocks in Scratch to wiring up a micro:bit, every session turns an idea into something you can see, touch, and control.

No experience needed — just curiosity. Our mentors guide every step, from your very first animation to your first working robot.

Club Activities

🎮

Scratch Animations

Create stories, games, and animations using simple drag-and-drop blocks — a fun first step into coding.

🤖

LEGO & micro:bit Robotics

Build small robots and program them to move, sense, and react using LEGO kits and the micro:bit.

💡

Team Build Challenges

Work in small teams to solve a weekly challenge, then demo your build to the whole club.
